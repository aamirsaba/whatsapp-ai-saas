Cancellation Policy

Last Updated: August 30, 2026

📌 Quick Summary: You can cancel your subscription at any time. Your service continues until the end of your current billing period. No cancellation fees apply.

1. Your Right to Cancel

You may cancel your Universal AI SaaS subscription at any time, for any reason. There are no cancellation fees, penalties, or long-term contracts.

Our subscription tiers are billed in British Pounds (GBP):

  • Starter: £25/month
  • Growth: £35/month (Most Popular)
  • Business: £50/month

2. How to Cancel

  1. Log in to your dashboard at bot.aamirsaba.com/dashboard
  2. Navigate to the "Billing & Plans" section.
  3. Click the "Cancel Subscription" button and confirm.
  4. Alternatively, email us at info@aamirsaba.com with your registered email address.

3. What Happens After Cancellation

  • Your service continues until the end of your current paid billing period.
  • No further charges will be applied to your payment method.
  • Unused tokens expire at the end of the billing period and are not refunded.
  • Account data is retained for 30 days after cancellation, then permanently deleted.

4. Free Trial Cancellation

Our free trial lasts 7 days or until 10,000 tokens are consumed (whichever comes first). If you do not upgrade to a paid plan before the trial ends, your account will be automatically suspended. No payment information is required to start a trial.
